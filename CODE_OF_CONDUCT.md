# Code of Conduct

Starcat contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome constructive
technical disagreement, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or
disclosure of private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation
when behavior threatens contributors or users. Report conduct issues privately through
the contact method listed on [dong4j's GitHub profile](https://github.com/dong4j), or
email [dong4j@gmail.com](mailto:dong4j@gmail.com).
