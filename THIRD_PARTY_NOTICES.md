# Third-party notices

Starcat bundles third-party open-source components. The in-app **About → Credits** list is
the canonical registry:

`Starcat/Features/About/AboutView.swift` → `AboutDependency.all`

When you add an SPM package, vendored source, generated code, or embedded third-party
asset, register it there. See [`docs/5-规范/开源致谢同步-规范.md`](docs/5-规范/开源致谢同步-规范.md).

License texts that ship next to embedded resources include:

- `Starcat/Resources/Readme/mermaid-11.16.0.LICENSE.txt`
